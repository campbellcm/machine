Questions? Answers.

Do employees have to post?

No. Crewcast is opt in. Most teams get strong participation from interviews, easy drafts, and rewards rather than mandates.

Will posts sound like AI?

Drafts start from each person's own interview answers and past writing, so the stories and opinions are theirs. They can edit anything before it goes live.

How do rewards and bonuses work?

Your company decides whether to offer a reward, what it is, and how much. Pick the metric, like most impressions or most leads, and the time frame. When a challenge ends, an admin approves the winner and cash rewards are paid through your payroll, so they are handled like any other bonus.

Can people outside the company see our rewards?

No. Challenges, leaderboards, and reward amounts are visible only to your team. Posts do mention that the author works at your company, which is expected whenever employees promote their employer.

Who counts as a paid teammate?

Only people who opt in and connect at least one account. Admins and people who just view reports are free.

How is revenue attributed?

Each teammate shares personal trackable links. Clicks flow back to the post and person that drove them, and Crewcast syncs with HubSpot or Salesforce to credit leads and closed deals.
