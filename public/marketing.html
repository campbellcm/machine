What is Crewcast?

Crewcast brings employee social content into one workspace: a shared performance overview, a team roster, company-defined rewards, and AI-assisted drafting.

Which social channels does it support?

V1 focuses on LinkedIn and X. Each teammate manages their own social accounts. Live access, publishing, and analytics depend on completing provider setup and the permissions available to your company.

Does AI publish for me?

No. AI helps prepare content using approved context. Authors review their drafts and decide what to publish. Company review requirements stay part of the workflow.

Can drafts arrive in email, Slack, or iMessage?

Those delivery options are planned. You can save a delivery preference in the preview, but drafts are currently available inside the app. Live daily generation also requires company AI setup and approved source material.

Who pays for and fulfills the rewards?

Your company chooses the prizes, sets the rules, and handles fulfillment. Crewcast does not purchase prizes, transfer money, or book travel.

Can I try it before connecting my accounts?

Yes. The interactive demo uses fictional teammates and sample results. No social account is connected and no live AI request is made in the demo. The setup guide explains what is needed for a real workspace.
